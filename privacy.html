privacy.

Last updated 7 November 2026.

what this site collects

Nothing that identifies you. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and doesn't track you across other sites, so there's no cookie banner.

when you get in touch

If you email or message us, we use your name, contact details and event details only to reply and to plan your booking. We don't sell or share them.

photos and video

Work shown here and on Instagram is shared with the client's permission. If you're in a photo and want it taken down, tell us and we'll remove it.

your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you at any time. Email hello@naavcaptured.com.
